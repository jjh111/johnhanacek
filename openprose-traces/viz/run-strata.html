How this page reads

One run of a program laid out across its strata, from caller inputs at the bottom to the delivered output at the top.

Modes
  • Flat — one stratum at a time as a reading slide (the top stratum is the deliverable · eval).
  • Tilted — the whole run as a 3D stack, strata laid up in time.
Navigating
  • Left rail: click a stratum chip (T1, T2, …) to jump, or hover the rail to read each stratum’s name.
  • Tilted: click a node to open its detail modal; hover for a quick preview. Prev/Next chips in the modal walk every node in the run.
  • Camera presets sit at the bottom: Pitched, Top-down, Side.
  • The Delta ledger on the right narrates stratum → stratum; click a row to jump.
Keys
  • ↑ ↓ advance / retreat the active stratum
  • ← → toggle flat / tilted (or walk nodes while a modal is open)
  • 1…5 jump to a stratum
  • Esc close the node modal
