Founding Design & Product

For teams building AI-native products, spatial computing, and robotics: from workshop to shipped product.

I take on founding designer and design engineering engagements for teams building AI-native products, spatial computing, and robotics. Same hands-on approach as the coaching: we build real things, and you keep them.

What that has looked like

  • Nanome 2022–2024

    Lead XR Product Designer. Shipped an XR + AI molecular interface to pharmaceutical customers; multiple rounds of usability testing with scientists led a navigation pivot — Spotlight/Follow replaced teleportation. Read the Nanome 2 case study.

    Two-month founding design: brand, five logos, and 37 live homepage approaches across 471 commits, distilled to one canonical style. Read the OpenProse case study.
