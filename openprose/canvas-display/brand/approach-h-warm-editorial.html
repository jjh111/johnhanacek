How it works

What is OpenProse?

You write responsibilities in plain language. Each responsibility describes an outcome you care about, not the steps to achieve it. Agents read your .prose files, coordinate with each other, and deliver results on the cadence you set.

No imperative scripts. No brittle pipelines. Just a clear statement of what should be true, and a runtime that makes it so.

.prose file 
# team-health.prose

responsibility "Weekly team pulse"
  owner:     engineering-ops
  cadence:   every Monday at 9am
  outcome:   "Collect sentiment from each team member,
             flag any scores below 6, and post a
             summary to #eng-health with trends."

responsibility "Dependency audit"
  owner:     security
  cadence:   daily
  outcome:   "Scan all repos for outdated or vulnerable
             dependencies. Open PRs for safe updates.
             Escalate critical CVEs to #security."
The open source stack

Three layers, one system

OpenProse is a complete stack for responsibility-driven orchestration. Each layer is independently useful, and together they form a coherent whole.

Layer 01

Language

How you describe work. A structured format for declaring responsibilities, owners, cadences, and expected outcomes. Human-readable, machine-executable.

Layer 02

Framework

How agents coordinate. The orchestration layer that reads your responsibility files, assigns work to capable agents, and manages handoffs between them.

Layer 03

Runtime

Where work executes. A sandboxed environment that runs agent tasks, enforces permissions, tracks progress, and delivers results back to the people who care.

Case study

Meridian Energy Systems

Anomaly detection across 14,000 sensors

Industrial IoT monitoring for distributed power infrastructure

Energy & Infrastructure

Meridian's reliability team spent 20 hours per week manually reviewing sensor data, triaging alerts, and coordinating responses across regional offices. Most of that time went to context-switching between dashboards and writing incident summaries.

With OpenProse, they defined three responsibilities: anomaly detection, threshold alerting, and weekly reliability reporting. The agents now monitor all 14,000 sensors continuously, flag deviations in real time, and deliver formatted reports every Monday.
