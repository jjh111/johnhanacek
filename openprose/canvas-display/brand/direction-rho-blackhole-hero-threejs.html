// ρ Black Hole — three.js fork · 2026-05-22

The shadow is a boundary — and the trace is the program.

This fork swaps the prior canvas-2D pipeline for the three.js leapfrog ray-tracer from vlwkaos/threejs-blackhole. Every pixel integrates a null geodesic around a Schwarzschild metric in a fragment shader, samples a milky-way background, blackbody-tinted stars, and an accretion-disk texture — then UnrealBloom carries the disk's glow. The void only exists when the operator asks for it: click anywhere on the canvas and the camera anchors to the angle of the click, the void fades in, holds, fades back out. The camera never moves. Tunables for rate and camera live in the panel at the right.
