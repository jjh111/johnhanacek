Three steps

From intent to outcome in three moves.

No templates. No prompts to maintain. Plain-language responsibilities, version-controlled, reviewable in any pull request.

01

Write the responsibility

Describe what needs to happen in plain language. requires: for inputs, ensures: for what must be true after a run. Fifteen lines is enough.

02

Set the contract

Define what good looks like. Accuracy thresholds, tone guidelines, guardrails, cadence. The contract is the quality guarantee — not the prompt.

03

Results arrive

Work completes on cadence, scored against the contract. Briefs land in Slack. Reports arrive via email. The responsibility runs continuously.
