I. Open source

Declarative contracts for the AI session.

A Prose program is a Markdown file with a contract — requires, ensures, strategies — not a chain of prompts. Forme auto-wires services by semantic match; any Prose Complete harness (Claude Code, Codex CLI, …) executes it.

The program declares what, not how, so it gets better as models improve. All MIT.

Common questions

Answers before the call.

What is OpenProse?

A service-as-software company. You pay a flat monthly fee and work gets done — reports generated, leads enriched, customer outreach drafted, operational briefs delivered — without anyone on your team opening a dashboard. The work runs on Prose programs, our open-source language for AI sessions.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Claude?

ChatGPT and Claude are tools you use. OpenProse is a service you receive. Your team doesn't prompt, doesn't configure, doesn't babysit. Outcomes arrive in your Slack, email, or the systems you already use — on a schedule, with human review where it matters.

How is this different from hiring someone?

Hiring takes weeks to source, months to ramp, and commits you to a full role even when you only need three specific responsibilities handled. OpenProse takes those responsibilities off your plate in days, runs them on a schedule with measurable confidence, and prices transparently — no salary band, no severance, no PTO.

What does a pilot look like?

We build one or two programs tailored to your team, run them for you, and deliver the output to Slack or email. Flat monthly fee, roughly $2,000 for two programs during pilot.

What if I want to run this in-house later?

You can. Prose is MIT-licensed and your programs live in a repo we share with you. Run them locally, fork them, hand them to someone else. The lock-in is quality of operation, not proprietary infrastructure.

Who reviews the output?

Every output passes through an automated review model before delivery. For work that goes externally to your customers, suppliers, or partners, we configure a human review step as part of the program.

How do you handle our data?

Your data stays in your org by default. OpenProse learns from what works across the platform — which program structures succeed, which patterns scale — but customer data never leaves your walls. Full tenant isolation is available for customers whose legal teams require it.

Who is OpenProse for?

Executives and operators at companies where the same operational work gets redone every week. Sales, marketing, support, research, product — any function with a repeatable pain that today gets handled by a person or gets dropped. We sell to the P&L owner, not to the individual whose job gets easier.

Versus LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen?

Those are libraries developers use to build agent systems. OpenProse is a company that delivers finished work product. We use our own open-source stack (Prose, Forme, Press) to run your programs; you never see the plumbing.

Can I see how you run your own company?

Yes — OpenProse runs on OpenProse. Our marketing, GTM, enrichment, and ops functions are Prose programs. The dogfood is the clearest picture of how the platform works in practice.
