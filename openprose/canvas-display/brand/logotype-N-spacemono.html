VI voice three registers
short
Write the outcome. We run it.

The tagline. Six words. Imperative + promise — verb + verb. No marketing softeners.

spec
Billed by delivery. If the outcome doesn't ship, no line.

Terms-of-art, plainly. Sentence-case, one full stop. The hardest claim is left undefended.

trace
Verified 11/11 checks. Delivered 09:14 ET.

Operational voice. Status, time, count. Reads like a log line because that's what it is.

# field-notes · 2026-04-30 · maya@ Once, the week began with a scramble. Someone opened seven dashboards, pulled the numbers, wrote the digest, and dropped it in the 09:00 standup. The rotation was a line in the budget. The standup was a line in the calendar. Nobody was quite sure what either accomplished beyond confirming that the dashboards were fine. Then the outcome was declared in a file. Fifteen lines. Every Monday at 09:00 ET, the digest arrives — verified, signed, archived. The standup happens anyway, but it is fifteen minutes shorter. Nobody asks where the numbers came from. They are already in the document. # the result is the point.
