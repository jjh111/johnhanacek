Three steps

From intent to outcome in three moves

01

Write the responsibility

Describe what needs to happen in plain language. No templates, no config files. Just declare the outcome you need in a .prose file.

02

Define what good looks like. Accuracy thresholds, tone guidelines, guardrails, cadence. The contract is the quality guarantee.

03

Results arrive

Work completes on schedule. Briefs land in Slack, reports arrive via email, dashboards update. The responsibility is fulfilled continuously.

Case study

Energy monitoring at Ridgeline Properties

How a 40-building portfolio replaced 20 hours of weekly reporting with a single responsibility file.

The situation

Ridgeline Properties manages 40 commercial buildings. Every week, an analyst spent 20 hours pulling utility data, cross-referencing weather patterns, and writing executive summaries. The process was manual, inconsistent, and always late.

# ridgeline-energy.prose responsibility "Portfolio Energy Monitor" owner facilities-team cadence every Monday 8am ET gather utility-api # 40 building meters weather-api # heating/cooling degree-days budget-sheet # annual energy budget deliver executive-brief "Slack #energy-ops" anomaly-alerts "email facilities-lead" full-report "PDF to SharePoint" contract accuracy "within 2% of manual audit" coverage "all 40 buildings, no gaps" tone "executive-friendly, flag anomalies"
