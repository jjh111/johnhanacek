01 — The concept
A self-maintaining book of responsibilities.
Write what needs to happen. The book keeps itself up.

You have responsibilities you want to offload. Today your options are: hire someone, or buy a tool and learn to use it. Both cost you attention.

OpenProse is a third option. You describe the responsibilities: what needs to happen, what good looks like, what the guardrails are. It handles them. No dashboard to check. No tool to learn. Results show up in Slack or email on a cadence.

We're not selling a tool. We're offloading a responsibility.
