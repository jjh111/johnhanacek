// ρ Black Hole — direction seventeen

A program is a boundary.

The shadow above is one identity in two voices — signet inside the glass, the canvas underneath. Click the void; let the hole open. Watch the disk wrap over the top of the shadow as you fall in (the back of the disk lensed around the photon sphere); listen for the silence past the horizon. The fall always ends in stillness, and the stillness always ends in another click.

Every aspect of the rendering is on a slider — event-horizon radius, photon ring sharpness, disk colour balance, blueshift, lensing strength, fall duration, starfield density, post-black hold. The chrome paradigm is borrowed from μ Bloom; the palette is its inversion. The art is primary; the physics is a guide, not a chain.
