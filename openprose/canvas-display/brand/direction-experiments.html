III.2 the three-part contract

The homepage's first section: what a responsibility requires, maintains, and is free to choose.

01 / requires

It states what it requires.

Preconditions, up front. What must hold before it runs: inputs, access, state. Nothing fires against an unmet world.

02 / maintains

It is judged on what it maintains.

The truth, kept current. What must stay true while it runs. Each run is scored against it: below the bar, the run holds; at or above, it ships.

03 / strategies

It is free in its strategies.

How it works, in prose, versioned. The approaches an agent may take to meet the contract, declared in the source.
