III The Constants

Two things never change across the menu, and they are what let the difference read cleanly: the metric grid every face is aligned to, and the palette every value is drawn from. Cap-height normalised. Each face below renders so its cap-height equals 0.70 × font-size, via font-size-adjust: cap-height 0.70. Without this, Kecal sits about 9% taller than the monos at the same nominal size. Measured in the browser: Kecal's natural cap-ratio is 0.73, the monos' is 0.67. The horizontal rules below mark the shared cap-line and the shared baseline. Every "H" reaches both.

The Kecal "x" inside each cell sits at the same cap-height as its sibling mono "H". This is what the pair display does on every tile below. Body text uses the same trick on x-height (font-size-adjust: ex-height 0.50) so lowercase letters share their waist line across families.

Greyscale palette. Every value on this page comes from one warm-brown ink (#2a2520) diluted to five alphas, mirrored on the dark surface by one cream tone (#fbf7ef) at the same five alphas. No neutral grey appears anywhere. This keeps the sepia warmth consistent under inversion.

IV The Accent Posture Axis

The accent posture is its own axis. The role stays constant: mono body, Kecal accent. The weight stays constant: 400 body, 800 accent. Four treatments exist: no italic, italic accent, italic display, both italic. Upright is the default (column 1, per c_a965130b). Italic is the historical option (column 2). Bold alone is the safest mark; bold-italic the loudest; an italic body with an upright bold accent flips the convention for a quieter, document-like stress. * JetBrains ships italics only to 600, so its bold-italic accent sits at 600 italic; Space ships 400 / 700 only, so its display rests at 400.
