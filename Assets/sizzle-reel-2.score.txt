# SIZZLE REEL · CUT 2 · THE MUSIC
#
# The music for Assets/sizzle-reel-2.script.txt (a script's music is the .score.txt with its
# name). All synth: every sound is made here from numbers. No samples.
#
# It is set up the way a music program is, on its own bars (4/4 at 120 BPM: a bar is 2 s, and
# bar 1 starts on the reel's first frame):
#   SOUNDS  SYNTH and DRUM blocks: how each sound is made
#   PARTS   PART blocks: drums, bass, chords, arp, lead, fx, and each one's pads, the patterns
#           it can play (a bar of steps for one or more of its sounds, or a tune)
#   CLIPS   CLIP lines: a part plays one of its pads over a stretch of bars. The timeline (E)
#           shows them on the bars under the scenes: drag one, its edges or its fades
#
# CHORDS   F C G Am goes round from bar 1, one a bar (Am:2 holds two); "CHORDS 30-32 Am" gives
#          those bars their own
# SYNTH    voice <wave> <cents> <level> (sine, triangle, square, saw, noise, harmonics; 1200
#          cents is an octave), filter <type> <Hz> <Q>, env <attack> <decay> <sustain> <release>,
#          lfo <gain|filter|pitch> <Hz> <depth>; play chord|root|arp <octave> (on a pad's steps),
#          notes (a pad's tune, or a take), rise (a sweep up across its clip); len: a note's most
#          steps; harmonics: each harmonic's level in dB against the first (the harmonics wave)
# DRUM     kind kick|snare|clap|hat|openhat|shaker|tick|boom|crash, tune (Hz), decay (s), tone (Hz)
# mix      level 0-1, pan -1 to 1, send <reverb> <delay>
# pad      <name> [<sound>] <steps or tune> [<sound> <steps>]... [len <steps>] [once]
#          steps: sixteen a bar, X loud, x, o soft, . rest. A pad goes round on the bars; a
#          "once" pad plays once from its clip's start; "pad <name> take <take>" sings a TAKE
# TAKE     a melody, a line a note, each keeping what was sung for it (scripts/reel-sing.py reads
#          a recording into one; "out" leaves a sung note out, a note with nothing sung was
#          added); its own lines map it onto the score: octave, shift, straighten, nuance, feel
# CLIP     <part> <bars> <pad> [<level>] [in <beats>] [out <beats>]
#          bars: 5 (bar 5), 5-8 (bars 5 to 8), 4.4-6 (bar 4 beat 4 to the end of bar 6)
# on       a sound effect plays at every moment of its kind: key, space, enter, clear (the
#          select-all before the next question), cut, item, beat (an @)
#
# CHECK    node scripts/reel-music.js check      the arrangement as a chart on the bars
