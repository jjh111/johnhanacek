# SIZZLE REEL · CUT 2 · THE MUSIC
#
# The score for Assets/sizzle-reel-2.script.txt: a script's music is the .score.txt with
# its name. Every sound is made here, by synths and drum voices built from numbers, on
# the script's own 120 BPM grid. There are no samples.
#
# ONE PIECE The music is one piece across the whole cut, on one clock: bars (four beats,
#           2 s) and steps (sixteen a bar) count from the reel's first frame, and a steps
#           pattern runs on that clock, so the groove never starts again at a cut. Each
#           SECTION starts on the bar line nearest its scene's cut when that is within a
#           beat of it, else on the cut itself, and the seam between two sections is
#           crossed the way the later one's "into" says. A note rings on past its section.
#
#   Am F C G  the arc: a call on a bell over Am (the open), a swell into the title, the
#             groove arriving on a downbeat (the answer), a lift (the command), a build into
#             the chorus and its hook on the lead (the results), a second verse (the feature,
#             the logos), a breakdown that leans on E (the quotes), a build into the last
#             chorus (the offer), and a drop into the end, where the bell's call resolves on A.
#
# SYNTH     A voice-built instrument (the patch vocabulary of like-every-cloud's
#           SynthVoice): "voice <wave> <cents> <level>" per oscillator (sine, triangle,
#           square, saw, noise; 1200 cents is an octave), then "filter <type> <Hz> <Q>",
#           "env <attack> <decay> <sustain> <release>" (seconds, sustain 0-1) and
#           "lfo <gain|filter|pitch> <Hz> <depth>". "play" says what it plays:
#             chord <octave>        the chord, held until it changes
#             root <octave>         the chord's root on the steps
#             arp <octave> <up|down|updown>   the chord's notes in turn on the steps
#             notes                 the "notes" line, one per step (. rests), from the
#                                   section's start: a melody starts with its section
#             hit <octave>          the chord once, on the section's cut
#             rise <beats>          a sweep up into the end of the section
#             chime <octave>        the chord rolled upwards (a sound effect)
#             note <name>           one note (a sound effect)
# DRUM      A drum voice: "kind" kick, snare, clap, hat, openhat, shaker, tick, boom or
#           crash; "tune" (Hz), "decay" (s), "tone" (Hz). A drum with no steps is a hit: it
#           lands on its section's cut (after a build or a drop, on the arrival).
# STEPS     Sixteen to a bar, a sixteenth is 0.125 s: X loud, x, o soft, . rest. A pattern
#           loops on the reel's own bars.
# MIX       "level" 0-1, "pan" -1 left to 1 right, "send <reverb> <delay>" 0-1 each.
# SFX       Sound effects the edit plays itself: "on key|space|enter|clear|cut|item|beat"
#           plays a SYNTH or DRUM voice at every such moment of the reel (each key of a
#           typed question, its Enter, the select-all before the next one, ...).
# FX        reverb (size s, decay, tone Hz, return), delay (time in beats, feedback, tone
#           Hz, return), tape (wow, flutter, age Hz, hiss), drive (amount), comp (threshold
#           dB, ratio, attack, release), master (level, fade in s, fade out s).
# SECTION   One per scene of the script, by its kind or its number:
#             play      the tracks it plays (name:0.5 plays one at half level); a level is
#                       where the instrument settles in this section
#             chords    from the section's start, one a bar (Am:2 holds two); else CHORDS
#             into      how the music arrives here from the section before:
#                         fade [beats]   a crossfade across the seam (2 beats)
#                         swell [beats]  in over the beats before it (4)
#                         build [beats]  a fill on the snare and a rise into it, a crash and
#                                        its hits on it (4)
#                         drop [beats]   the drums and the bass go silent for the beats
#                                        before it, then a crash (1)
#                         cut            everything changes on it
#             start     auto (the default), bar (the nearest bar line) or cut (the cut)
#             sweep     <from Hz> <to Hz> filters the music across it
#
# PLAY      node scripts/reel-dev.mjs, open the page it prints, press E (the timeline: the
#           music under the scenes) or M (the synth rack). Every knob, step and cell there
#           edits a line of this file. Saving this file from any editor plays it at once.
# CHECK     node scripts/reel-music.js check      the arrangement: sections, seams, notes
# FILM      node scripts/render-sizzle-reel.mjs --cut=2 renders it into the MP4.

TEMPO  120
KEY    A minor

# ── the band ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SYNTH pad
  voice    saw -9 0.45
  voice    saw 9 0.45
  voice    sine -1200 0.35
  filter   lowpass 1300 0.7
  env      0.7 0.6 0.75 1.4
  lfo      filter 0.18 380
  play     chord 3
  level    0.1
  send     0.55 0.12

SYNTH bass

  play     root 2
  steps    x..x..x.x..x.x..
  len      2
  level    0.33
  pan      0
  send     0 0

SYNTH arp

  env      0.002 0.16 0 0.12
  play     arp 4 updown
  steps    x.xx.x.xx.xx.x.x
  level    0.24
  pan      0.25
  send     0.3 0.4

  send     0.6 0.5
