# SIZZLE REEL · CUT 2 · THE MUSIC
#
# The music for Assets/sizzle-reel-2.script.txt (a script's music is the .score.txt with its
# name). All synth: every sound is made here from numbers, on the script's 120 BPM grid.
#
#   kick clap hat   the beat
#   bass            the chord's root, on the eighths
#   chords          the chord, held for its bar
#   lead            the tune: four bars, once round the chords
#   keys enter select pop   the sound effects: the edit plays these itself
#
# ONE LOOP  Everything goes round on the reel's own bars (a bar is four beats, 2 s), from its
#           first frame to its last: the chords (CHORDS, one a bar), the steps and the tune. A
#           cut never starts anything again. A SECTION (one per scene) only says which parts
#           play there, how loud, and how the music crosses into it.
#
# SYNTH     voice <wave> <cents> <level>   one line per oscillator: sine, triangle, square,
#                                          saw or noise; 1200 cents is an octave up
#           filter <type> <Hz> <Q>  env <attack> <decay> <sustain> <release>  (seconds)
#           lfo <gain|filter|pitch> <Hz> <depth>
#           play chord <octave>     the chord, held for its bar
#                root <octave>      the chord's root, on the steps
#                notes              the notes line, one a step (. rests), going round from 0;
#                                   a note lasts until the next one, len steps at most
#                arp, hit, rise, chime, note: see scripts/reel-music.js
# DRUM      kind kick|snare|clap|hat|openhat|shaker|tick|boom|crash, tune (Hz), decay (s),
#           tone (Hz)
# steps     sixteen to a bar: X loud, x, o soft, . rest
# level     0-1;  pan  -1 left to 1 right;  send <reverb> <delay>  0-1 each
# on        a sound effect plays at every moment of its kind: key (each letter typed), space,
#           enter, clear (the select-all before the next question), cut, item, beat (an @)
# SECTION   play     the parts it plays; bass:0.6 plays the bass at 0.6 of its level
#           into     how the music arrives here: fade (a crossfade, the default), swell, build
#                    (a fill on the clap into it), drop (the drums and bass rest for a beat
#                    before it) or cut; a number after it is its length in beats
#           chords   its own chords instead of the loop's (the end holds Am)
#           sweep    <from Hz> <to Hz>  a filter over the music across it
#           start    auto (on the bar line nearest the cut when that is a beat away or less),
#                    bar or cut
#
# PLAY      node scripts/reel-dev.mjs, then E (the timeline: every part's notes under the scenes)
#           or M (the synth rack). Every control edits a line of this file.
# CHECK     node scripts/reel-music.js check
