# SIZZLE REEL · CUT 2
#
# This file is the edit: every word on screen and when it happens. The page
# Assets/sizzle-reel-2.html plays it, and node scripts/render-sizzle-reel.mjs --cut=2
# films it. Nothing else holds the words or the timing.
#
# WORDS     One line on screen is one line here: change the words after the field name.
#           <em>…</em> turns words gold. " | " separates the parts of one field.
# TIMING    "SCENE answer 4.5" is a scene 4.5 s long. "@2.35" is a beat 2.35 s into its
#           scene or ITEM. Change a length and everything after it moves with it.
#           The grid is 120 BPM: a beat is 0.5 s, a bar 2 s. The cut is made to run
#           exactly 60.0 s, and check prints the total after a change.
# PICTURES  In a beat, "video" or "img" names the file and "from" is the second of the
#           clip it starts at. "loop" repeats a short clip. "zoom" runs from one scale
#           to another, "focus" is the point it zooms on (0-1 across, 0-1 down). A beat's
#           picture dissolves in over the last one; "cut" brings it in at once, on its
#           time (the OpenProse finalists flip by, an eighth each). A beat with only a
#           picture keeps the words before it. An answer's "img" is the portrait beside
#           it, in the About page's oval.
# TIERS     "tiers" names what found the answer; under the bar each is drawn as a mark,
#           not a word: BM25 (keyword), MiniLM (semantic), LFM2.5 · WebGPU (a model),
#           scene language, intent. Any other name is a dot.
# CUES      A scene's transitions. "cue in 0.2" brings the answer 0.2 s after the
#           question's Enter and "cue arrive 0.3" has it all arrive in 0.3 s (the rig's
#           own is 0.6); "cue out 0.5" starts its content leaving 0.5 s before the scene
#           ends and "cue leave 0.15" has it gone in 0.15 s (own 0.3); "cue ease cubic"
#           is the curve it moves on (own, expo, cubic, sine, back, linear); "cue typing
#           30" types the question at 30 characters a second. A cue line above the first
#           SCENE sets it for every scene; a scene's own still wins. No cues: the rig's
#           own timing.
# SHOTS     The scenes go in any order: each takes its lines, beats, cues and fish lines
#           with it. S in the preview opens the shot list: drag a shot to move it, set its
#           transitions, copy it or take it out. The music stays on its bars.
# CHECK     node scripts/reel-script.js check   the cue sheet, the total, any mistakes
#           node scripts/reel-script.js fmt     refresh the [bracketed] timecodes
# PREVIEW   node scripts/reel-dev.mjs and open the page it prints. E opens the timeline:
#           drag a scene's edge, a beat or an exit mark, or click a scene to change its
#           words. Each change saves this file and the preview comes back at the same
#           moment; saving this file from any editor reloads it too. [ and ] jump a
#           scene, L loops one, space pauses (and stays paused across saves).
# MUSIC     sizzle-reel-2.score.txt, next to this file, is the music: synth sounds, parts
#           with their pads, and clips on the music's own bars. M in the preview opens the
#           synth rack; the timeline's music lanes move, stretch and fade the clips.
# FISH      In a scene, "fish @1.5 big to 0.72 0.8" directs the fish from 1.5 s in: big
#           (the drawn fish), school or all. "to" a point (0-1 across, 0-1 down) where it
#           stays, or auto (the scene's own spot); "look" at a point, auto (what the scene
#           shows) or off; "idle" hover, sweep, circle, wander or auto; "pace" 0.3 to 3.
#           Each holds until a later line of its kind for that fish, and no further than
#           its scene's end, so a scene keeps its own fish; end one with "carry" to run on
#           past the cut. "big auto" gives all of it back to the reel's own. Once, at their
#           time: dart and turn (the big fish), scatter and regroup (the school), "feed 0.4
#           0.85" (food). F in the preview opens the Fish panel; the timeline's fish lanes
#           show what each fish looks at and does across the cut.
#
# The copy is the site's own (search chunks, resume.json, the media-kit boards), under
# the boards' evidence rule, and quotes are verbatim. No email on screen: the offer and
# the end card send people to the site. A line starting with # is a note.
