# Inspirations

## The Talks

### Bret Victor — Media for Thinking the Unthinkable

MIT Media Lab, 2013 · 39 min

A Nature network-theory paper redrawn so its algorithm runs in the margins. A circuit where every component carries a live plot of its own voltage and current. The closing list: see the behavior, see the whole state at once, change something and watch what responds, draw the representations.

> The authors have a very rich picture in their heads and they're trying to compress that picture to transmit over a very low bandwidth channel which is this stream of numbers and symbols.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HK77hLfRPEA

### Bret Victor — Stop Drawing Dead Fish

San Francisco, 2012 · 53 min

A drawn fish learns behaviors: swim, dart, surprise. It performs with his hand, 60 decisions a second, and there is no timeline in the piece. Closes with a digital puppet show set to music.

> Everything we draw should be alive by default.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZfytHvgHybA

### Ken Perlin — AR and the Future of Language

AR in Action, 2017 · 14 min

Draws working objects in the air mid-conversation. A design gets remixed by grabbing a curve off it, every drawn noun doubles as a verb, and the last demo is a drawn bird that is ticklish.

> The most important thing about these interactive procedural characters is not that we are interested in them. The most important thing is that they are interested in us.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZDkHgP45Tqg

### Ken Perlin — The Future Evolution of Language

UIST 2018, Berlin · 32 min

Two fish drawn with different gestures move differently, the drawing itself the instruction. Kids under seven evolve every language. The close: we are not natives of the worlds we are building.

> All we can do in any one generation is create possibilities, because we are strangers in a strange land. We are not natives. So we need to provide possibilities to children about the world they will make.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZZi3I_NCiUk
